### 🎞️ Importing to DaVinci Resolve 1. Extract the .zip. You will see a folder of numbered PNGs. 2. In Resolve's Media Storage, right-click the folder and select "Import as Sequence". 3. If Resolve imports them as 100 individual photos, go to the Media Pool options (three dots) and ensure "Show Individual Frames" is OFF. 4. Drag the resulting clip to your timeline. It will treat the sequence as a single 4-second video with transparency.
